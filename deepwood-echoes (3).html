DEEPWOOD ECHOES

a 2D survival prototype
Your transport went down over the Deepwood three nights ago. You're alive. Most of the others weren't. Somewhere out here is Mira — a woman who's been surviving this forest far longer than she should have — and she'll tell you what needs doing, one task at a time.

Hunger, Thirst, Warmth and Sanity decay constantly — let any collapse and your body starts failing. WASD move  •  E gather/mine/drink  •  F build or feed a campfire  •  SPACE fight or hunt the nearest creature  •  SHIFT sprint  •  R cook raw meat at a fire  •  1 eat berries  •  2 drink water  •  3 eat raw meat (fills you up, but it's bad for you)  •  4 eat cooked meat  •  P pause, view skills, and craft weapons. Reach Torchcraft level 2 to make a torch with T. Drink from the pond edge without entering the water. Reach Swimming level 3 to cross ponds. On mobile, drag controls, maps, and dialogs with two fingers.

Chop wood, forage, mine stone, iron and copper, and fight to train four skills — they raise your yields, cut your fuel costs, and unlock better weapons. Wolves hunt in packs; tigers and bears start showing up after the first few nights. Rabbits and deer can be hunted for raw meat — cook it at a fire before eating or it'll cost you. Your fire is what keeps predators off you — if it dies (and heavy rain will put it out) they will close in. Rain also chills you when you are away from a fire.

Complete everything Mira asks of you before the sixth dawn and you'll get your signal out. Fail to, and even if your body is still standing, you don't make it home.
📱 Mobile tip: During play, use two fingers to drag the joystick zone, action buttons, minimap, mission box, and dialogs anywhere on screen — reposition them to fit your hands.
